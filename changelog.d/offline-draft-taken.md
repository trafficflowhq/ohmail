### A draft saved while offline belongs to its message when it reaches your account

On the web and the desktop, a reply or message written without a connection is saved as a draft once the connection is back, and the reply or message on screen now keeps using that draft. Before, a second reply written offline could be sent without it, and its draft stayed in Drafts after the reply went, one press away from a second copy. A draft saved twice while offline now ends up as one, and a draft that was already on its way when you pressed Send is removed once the message has gone.
