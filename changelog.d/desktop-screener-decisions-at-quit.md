### Screener decisions made just before quitting are no longer lost

Screener decisions made just before quitting are no longer lost, and the Screener count no longer flashes them
at the next start. On a desktop app signed in to your own server, or to a Cloud account whose sign-in address is
not one of its mailboxes, a decision pressed in the seconds before you quit was dropped, and the sender waited
again after the next start; it is now carried out when the app opens again, and so is a delete or a move
pressed in that moment. At that start the sidebar's
Screener count could also show those senders for a moment before the decision reached the server.
