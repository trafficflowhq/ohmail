### A message with a long To list turns read when you read it

A message sent to many people kept its unread dot in the Ohbox, and the blue line beside it in the
reader, after you had read it: a To or Cc list taller than the reader kept its sender line and its
first lines from being on screen at the same moment. The read rule is now one and the same for every
message on web, desktop, ohmail Cloud and the phone, which gains it too: A message turns read once its
sender line has been fully on screen (a preview counts) and its first lines have stayed on screen for
0.7 s, in either order. Reaching the end is not needed. Nothing that was never on screen turns read;
Mark unread starts it over. On web and desktop the 0.7 s count starts once you open the message,
press in it or scroll the reader yourself, so a message you only pass with j and k stays unread.
