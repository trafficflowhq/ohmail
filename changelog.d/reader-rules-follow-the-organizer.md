### Rules made on the computer that organizes your mail show on your other devices

On a device that only reads a mailbox another computer organizes, Settings → Rules now lists that
computer's rules, including ones made or changed there, instead of only the rules this device wrote
(desktop, web). A rule made there is listed without Change or Revoke and says to change it there. A
change you make here shows as you made it until that computer's next copy includes it; an organizing
computer on an older release is followed once its copy agrees. On such a mailbox the sender sheet's
"Their rules" no longer names an older rule of this device beside the one that files the mail
(desktop, web, phone).
