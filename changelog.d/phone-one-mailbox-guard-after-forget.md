### The phone opens a second mailbox only once the first one is removed

On a phone that organizes its own mailbox, connecting a different mailbox while the first one's mail was
still kept on the phone could go through: after a Cloud pairing, a switch to another account, or a Stop and
remove that was not confirmed. Both mailboxes then shared one store, so the second one's Screener listed the
first one's letters and the phone counted both mailboxes' mail together. The phone now checks its own store
before it connects anything. Connecting a different mailbox is refused with "This phone already has a
mailbox open", nothing is written, signed in to or deleted, and the kept mailbox is listed in Servers so
Stop and remove can take it. The same mailbox still opens again as before, and opening the kept mailbox
after a refused Connect no longer starts organizing it on the phone by itself. A phone whose store an
earlier version had already filled with two mailboxes opens neither and says so, and Stop and remove
clears it. On Android and iPhone alike.
