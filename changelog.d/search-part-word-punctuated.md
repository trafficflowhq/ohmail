### The start of an address, a path or a number finds its message

The start of an address, a host, a path, a file name, a version or a decimal (`desk@site.exa`, `docs/set`,
`report.fin`, `3.14`) now finds the messages that hold the whole of it, bodies included, from its fourth
character, on ohmail Cloud, your own server, the desktop and the phone. A search of more than eight words
now still reads its last word as the start of a longer one.
