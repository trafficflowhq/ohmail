### Faster search for words found in some of your mail

A word or part of a word found in a few percent of your letters, such as a file name part like "scan"
or "invoice", answers sooner: the server reads its matches once and walks your newest mail against
them, where it walked thousands of letters or sorted every match to fill a page. A word in most of
your older letters but few recent ones reads each of them once, not twice. The results are the same.
On ohmail Cloud and your own server.
