### Pictures in newsletters sent through Klaviyo show again

A newsletter sent through Klaviyo, which many Shopify shops use, showed its logo and then a long
run of empty space: every other picture was refused as a tracker and drawn as a blank the width of
the letter. Klaviyo now serves those pictures from `cdn.klaviyomail.com`, and the whole of
`klaviyomail.com` was on the list of tracking hosts. The list now names Klaviyo's tracking hosts
(`klclick.com` and its numbered siblings) instead, so the pictures load and its open and click
tracking stays refused. On every app.
