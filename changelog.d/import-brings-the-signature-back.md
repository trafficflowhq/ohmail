### Import settings brings a reconnected mailbox's signature back where you can see it

After removing a mailbox and adding it again, Import settings restored the signature saved in the mailbox,
but Settings still said "No signature" and new messages went out without it until the window was
reloaded. Every open window now picks up the restored signature on its next sync, on desktop, web, phone
and Cloud.

Import settings also never loses a signature you typed on the mailbox: saved settings without a signature
leave yours as it is, and a different saved signature replaces yours only when the card you pressed Import
on said it would replace that one.
