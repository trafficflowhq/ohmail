### Setup right after the organizing computer quits asks who organizes, and a stood-down computer counts like the new organizer

On the desktop app, a new install set up within minutes of the organizing computer quitting or restarting
was told nobody organized the mailbox, asked nothing, and its Agree made it the organizer. Setup now reads
the moment the other computer let go from the mailbox's settings and shows "Already organized elsewhere",
with nothing pre-selected, for ten minutes after it quit; after that it says the organizer stopped. After
"Stop organizing here" the mailbox is free for another install at once, as before. An organizer working through a long stretch that moves no mail now keeps renewing its claim, so another
computer never reads it as gone while it is still organizing.

A computer that stopped organizing because another one took over showed a Screener count one or two above
the new organizer's for up to ten minutes, through a relaunch. It now reads the new organizer's settings
on its next pass, at launch and whenever the organizer changes, so both count the same.
