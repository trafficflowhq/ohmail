### A wide newsletter is fitted to the fold's cover screen on every open

On the phone, a newsletter built on a fixed 600 px table could open on a foldable's cover screen drawn at
the zoom it had on a wider screen: cut at the right edge and scrolling sideways, with no note. Each width
reading now carries the zoom it was drawn at, so a letter still wider than its pane is fitted again, and a
letter is never treated as finished while it is wider than the screen: it is drawn fitted, or shown as text
with the reason.
