### A draft open in two places no longer overwrites itself

On the web and the desktop, when a message you are writing is changed in another window or on another device, what you type here no longer silently replaces it. The draft keeps the other version, what you typed is kept as a new draft beside it, and the message says so. Send does not send the other version; "Send as a new letter" sends what is on screen. Your own saves, including ones made offline or retried after a lost connection, never count as a change elsewhere.

A send whose save was refused now says it was not sent, and no longer says your draft is kept when the draft holds different words.

On your own server, update the server before the web app and the desktop app; an older app keeps working as before.
