### A new Outlook history folds when the earlier message opens with its quote

0.30.18 folded a new Outlook for Mac reply's history whose earlier message quotes and answers a bullet only
when a line sat above the quote. When the earlier message opens with its quote box, as Outlook writes it with
no greeting, the whole history stayed on screen with no Show history. On the desktop, web and phone apps it
now folds at the reply header when that box is the message's only one, in its colour, does not end with the
sender's sign-off (alone or above a short signature block or phone line) and is answered below in a sentence,
and the message is signed below the answer with a closing that only ever ends a letter and the name of the
sender on the reply header's From line: "Viele Grüsse" over "Ben", "Mit freundlichen Grüssen, Ben Sample",
"Best regards, Ben". Everything else reads as in 0.30.18. A closing the writer could also be saying to the
sender ("Gruss Ben", "Ciao Ben", "Cheers Ben", "Thanks, Ben", "Danke und Gruss, Ben", "Merci, Ben") or the name
alone signs nothing, so a message signed that way stays open as in 0.30.18, even when its sender wrote it. The
cost, as under the other Outlook headers: the writer's text in the history's own colour, with no mark, typed
below such a quote in a message signed with a closing that only ever ends a letter and the sender's name,
folds with it.
