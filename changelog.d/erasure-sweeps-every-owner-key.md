### Deleting your account removes its preferences too, and a returning browser drops its copy

Delete your account removed the account's mail, drafts and decisions from the browser, but left the small
preferences kept under the account's id: the Search order, "Not now" on the sent-mail review, what the
reader does after a verb, the picture quality, and the open tab's notes about the account. The folder
branches opened in the rail and the messages kept in their original colours were kept for every account in
one place, where an erasure could not find them. On the web and in the desktop app an erasure now removes
all of these, whether it was pressed there or heard from the server; on the phone, the "Not now" choice goes
with the pairing.

A browser that came back after its account was deleted on another device learned of it on the page shown
before the mailbox opens, or on the sign-in page, and kept the account's mail and drafts on disk behind the
sign-in page. It now removes that account's copy before the sign-in page shows.

The opened folder branches and the original-colours choices are now kept per account, so after this update
they start closed and in the theme's colours once. Signing out and a session ended on another device behave
as before, and another account's data on the same browser is untouched.
